# About

This is a list of all our repositories related to NHP.
It may not be exhaustive because some repos are in development or may have been archived, for example.

We use [GitHub topics](https://github.com/topics) to help organise our repos and allow others to discover them.
All repos are in the [#new-hospital-programme](https://github.com/topics/new-hospital-programme) topic and each section below has its own topic.
Most are also assigned to [#nhp-operational](https://github.com/topics/nhp-operational) or [#nhp_reporting](https://github.com/topics/nhp-reporting), depending on their mode of use.

See [the repo interrelationships page](https://github.com/The-Strategy-Unit/nhp_products/wiki/Repo-interrelationships) of this wiki for details on how the repos relate to each other.
